# 90-SECOND LIVE DEMO & EVALUATION SCRIPT

**Target Audience**: Hack2Skill Judges & Technical Evaluators  
**Primary Goal**: Demonstrate 100% alignment with all 6 Problem Statement pillars in under 90 seconds with zero friction.

---

## ⏱ 0:00 – 0:15 | The Problem & Landing Hook
* **Action**: Open `/` (Landing Page).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Every year, final-year engineering students face a crisis: they pick trivial CRUD clones that examiners fail, or overambitious projects they abandon. We built **ProjectSpark** — an AI-powered platform that architects defensible capstone projects across 6 core pillars."*
* **Visual**: Point out the "Built for final-year builders" eyebrow and the 6 Problem Statement pillars summary.

---

## ⏱ 0:15 – 0:25 | Frictionless Evaluator Access
* **Action**: Click **Launch Guest Portal** or **Log in** (`/login`).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"We know judges have zero time to create accounts and verify emails. With one click, click '⚡ Continue as Guest Evaluator', and you enter with a pre-configured Final-Year Student profile (Alex Chen, B.Tech CSE)."*
* **Visual**: Showcase the animated ambient background glow, glassmorphic card, and instant 1-click transition.

---

## ⏱ 0:25 – 0:45 | Pillar 1: Interests & Skills Intake
* **Action**: Navigate to `/generate` (Intake Wizard).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Our intake is tailored specifically for academic capstones: students pick their domain passions (Computer Vision, AI, Healthcare), their technical skills across languages, frameworks, and AI runtimes, plus realistic submission deadlines and team formats."*
* **Visual**: Click "Computer Vision & Edge AI", select "Python + OpenCV + PyTorch", and click "Continue". Note the live match counter updating.

## ⏱ 0:45 – 1:15 | Pillars 2 through 6: The Capstone Blueprint
* **Action**: Select **AttendAI** (`/generate/results/attendai`).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Here is the core of our solution covering all remaining pillars:*
  > *1. **Pillar 2 (Idea)**: AttendAI with 96% fit scoring and transparent matching rationale.*
  > *2. **Pillar 3 (Features)**: Strict distinction between P0 Core MVP features (anti-spoofing, face recognition) and P1 Advanced Distinctions (SMS alerts).*
  > *3. **Pillar 4 (Technologies)**: Layered stack with explicit architectural justification for every tool chosen.*
  > *4. **Pillar 5 (Roadmap)**: An interactive 4-phase capstone timeline with review deliverables.*
  > *5. **Pillar 6 (Improvements & Viva Prep)**: Production hardening plus our exclusive **Viva Defense Kit** featuring real examiner questions, model answers, and defense strategies."*
* **Visual**:
  - Filter between P0 and P1 feature tabs.
  - Click a roadmap checkbox (interactive completion).
  - Open a question in the Viva Defense accordion.

---

## ⏱ 1:15 – 1:30 | Real-World Value & Deliverables
* **Action**: Click **"Download Synopsis (.md)"**, then navigate to `/dashboard`.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Students can download their formatted college project synopsis with one click for immediate university submission. In their dashboard, their Viva Readiness dial recalculates live as phases are completed. ProjectSpark turns vague ideas into defensible capstones."*
* **Visual**: File downloads immediately; dashboard displays live 84% Viva Readiness score.
