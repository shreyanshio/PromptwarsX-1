# 90-SECOND LIVE DEMO & PRESENTATION SCRIPT

**Primary Goal**: Demonstrate end-to-end alignment with the Problem Statement in under 90 seconds with zero friction.

---

## ⏱ 0:00 – 0:15 | The Problem & Landing Hook
* **Action**: Open `/` (Landing Page).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Every year, final-year engineering students face a crisis: they pick trivial CRUD clones that examiners reject, or overambitious projects they abandon. We built **ProjectSpark** — an AI-powered platform that architects practical, defensible capstone projects from intake to defense."*
* **Visual**: Point out the "Built for final-year builders" eyebrow, dynamic domain preview, and product architecture cards.

---

## ⏱ 0:15 – 0:25 | Instant Demo Access
* **Action**: Click **Launch Demo Workspace** or **Sign in** (`/login`).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"With one click, select 'Continue with Demo Account' to instantly load an active final-year engineering workspace without tedious registration barriers."*
* **Visual**: Showcase the animated ambient background glow, smooth tab switching, theme toggle, and instant 1-click transition.

---

## ⏱ 0:25 – 0:45 | Student Interests & Skills Intake
* **Action**: Navigate to `/generate` (Intake Wizard).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Our intake is tailored specifically for academic capstones: students pick their domain passions (Computer Vision, AI, Healthcare), their technical skills across languages, frameworks, and AI runtimes, plus realistic submission deadlines and team formats."*
* **Visual**: Click "Computer Vision & Edge AI", select "Python + OpenCV + PyTorch", and click "Continue". Note the live match counter updating.

---

## ⏱ 0:45 – 1:15 | The Capstone Blueprint Architect
* **Action**: Select **AttendAI** (`/generate/results/attendai`).
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Here is the core of our solution providing end-to-end guidance:*
  > *1. **Idea & Fit**: AttendAI with clear stack alignment and transparent matching rationale.*
  > *2. **Features**: Strict distinction between Core MVP features (anti-spoofing, face recognition) and Advanced Distinctions (SMS alerts).*
  > *3. **Technologies**: Layered stack with explicit architectural justification for every tool chosen.*
  > *4. **Roadmap**: An interactive 4-phase capstone timeline with review deliverables.*
  > *5. **Improvements & Defense Prep**: Production hardening plus our exclusive **Viva Defense Kit** featuring real examiner questions, model answers, and defense strategies."*
* **Visual**:
  - Filter between Core MVP and Distinction feature tabs.
  - Click a roadmap checkbox (interactive completion).
  - Open a question in the Viva Defense accordion.

---

## ⏱ 1:15 – 1:30 | Real-World Value & Deliverables
* **Action**: Click **"Download Synopsis (.md)"**, then navigate to `/dashboard`.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Students can download their formatted college project synopsis with one click for immediate university submission. In their dashboard, their Academic Defense Readiness dial recalculates live as milestones are completed. ProjectSpark turns vague ideas into defensible capstones."*
* **Visual**: File downloads immediately; dashboard displays live Academic Defense Readiness score.
